What can I play?

Links must point straight to a video file (MP4, WebM, MOV) or an HLS playlist (.m3u8). A web page address, such as a YouTube or Vimeo page, is not a video file and won’t play. Support depends on your browser’s codecs, and sites that don’t allow cross-origin access can block streams. Local files are played in your browser and never uploaded.

Shortcuts: Space or K play/pause, J / L skip 10 s, ← / → skip 5 s, ↑ / ↓ volume, M mute, F fullscreen, C subtitles, P Picture in Picture. You can also drop a video file onto the page.
